    javi · 1980 – today

    A life, in milestones.

    I'm Javi. These are the moments that made me, from a farmhouse in the Basque Country to today. Each cloud of dots is a memory and each galaxy a stage of my life: open one, follow it to the next, or look for a person, a place or a thread. Work is one of many. Two things come next: a book for my children and an AI you will be able to talk to.
