What would make it better.

Ideas nobody has approved yet, each drawn against what the site does today. A board is the proposal: when the creator approves one it becomes a task in the ROADMAP with the same ID, and the board is deleted when the change ships.

MILESTONE-MEDIACard

A photo or a sound at a milestone

Why. A few memories are better with a photograph or a recording. In the card a photo is drawn in dots first and sharpens on press; a sound shows its waveform and plays only when asked. Only where the creator has approved it.

Accept a memory file may name one local image and/or one audio file, both optional and public by approval; the image carries an alt, nothing loads before the card opens and nothing leaves the origin, sound never autoplays, the flat page shows the image and an audio control, and a test fails on any media for a memory not marked public.

CLOSED-MEMORIESScene · the depth of the book and the clone

Say how much more there is, with a number

Why. The repository is public, so nothing private can sit next to the open memories, not even as a stub. What the site can do is say how much exists: a count per age and a total, so a visitor feels the immensity behind what is told, which is what the book and the clone are made of.

Accept the creator decides whether to show the numbers and gives them; they load in both languages; the labels, the hero and the flat page read them from life.json; a galaxy is drawn exactly the same with and without them; AGENTS and the documents say there is one zone and no stubs.

BOOK-CHAPTERSScene · the book

The book as nodes

Why. The book is one ring today. As a run of chapter rings along the path ahead, each opens a card with its blurb and the memories it draws on, which are lit in every galaxy.

Accept chapters are listed in content/ in both languages with a title, a blurb and public memory ids, and appear only once the creator provides them (the repository holds no chapter title before); each ring is a station with its own card and lines to its memories, in the rail and the keyboard order, and the book's ring stays the last.

DREAM-ARCSScene · what was wished for

What was wished for, done and let go

Why. Some memories are the place where something was wished for and others where it came true. A dashed arc from one to the other, and a hollow ring for what is still pending, tells that story without a sentence.

Accept an optional wish field in a memory pointing at the memory where it came true (or pending), only for dreams the creator makes public; arcs draw in the sky when the wish memory is open, pending ones are hollow rings like the book and the clone, and a test fails on a pointer to a memory that does not exist.

MEMORY-TENSECopy · the tense of the memories

One tense for every memory, and one sentence to confirm

Why. The memories mix tenses: some tell the moment in the historic present (“At twelve I notice that girls are different”) and others in the past. The voice rules need one, in both languages, and the brand page's voice section says present for the site and past for a moment. One guide sentence also still asks the creator to confirm it.

Accept the creator chooses A or B and rules on the guide sentence. Then every memory in both languages is rewritten in that tense in one change, the voice section of design/index.html says it, and the creator approves each rewritten sentence, since it is his own voice.
